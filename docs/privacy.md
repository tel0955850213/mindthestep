# Privacy Policy · 隱私權政策

_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

MindTheStep (小心台階) is built to work on your iPhone without sending your data anywhere.

## English

**No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** MindTheStep does not collect personal information and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

**Camera and LiDAR.** Depth and camera frames are processed on your iPhone in real time to detect steps. They are never uploaded. When a step is detected, a small photo is saved **on your iPhone only** so you can review it in the Report tab.

**Location.** If you allow location access, each detected step is pinned on a map **stored on your iPhone only**. Location is never sent to us or to family alerts.

**Family alerts (optional).** If you turn on family alerts, the app sends a short message (the type of step, its height and the time) to a randomly generated channel on the third-party service [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh), so your family member can receive it. No location, photo, name or contact detail is included. Anyone who knows the channel name can read these messages, so share the setup link only with people you trust. You can turn family alerts off at any time in the Family tab.

**Apple Intelligence note.** The walk summary for your family is written by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is uploaded to write it. If you choose to send it, it is delivered the same way as family alerts.

**Sharing.** Reports are shared only when you tap Share, using the standard iOS share sheet.

**Deleting your data.** Tap "Clear all hazards" in the Report tab, or delete the app. All data is stored on your device.

**Children.** MindTheStep is not directed at children under 13.

**Not a medical device.** MindTheStep is an awareness aid. It does not replace a cane, a companion, or your own attention.

**Contact.** Please open an issue at https://github.com/tel0955850213/mindthestep/issues

## 繁體中文

**不需要帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析、不追蹤。** 小心台階不蒐集個人資料，也不會把資料提供給廣告商或資料仲介。

**相機與 LiDAR。** 相機與深度畫面只在你的 iPhone 上即時處理，用來偵測台階，從不上傳。偵測到台階時，會在**你的 iPhone 上**存一張小照片，方便你在「報告」分頁查看。

**位置。** 如果你允許取用位置，每個偵測到的台階會標在地圖上，**只存在你的 iPhone**。位置不會傳給我們，也不會包含在家人通知裡。

**家人通知（可選）。** 開啟家人通知後，App 會把一則簡短訊息（台階類型、高度和時間）傳到第三方服務 [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh) 上一個隨機產生的頻道，讓你的家人收到。訊息不含位置、照片、姓名或聯絡資料。知道頻道名稱的人都能看到這些訊息，請只把設定連結傳給你信任的人。你可以隨時在「家人」分頁關閉。

**Apple Intelligence 短訊。** 給家人的步行短訊由 Apple 的裝置端模型撰寫，撰寫過程不上傳任何資料。如果你選擇傳送，傳送方式與家人通知相同。

**分享。** 只有在你按下「分享」時，才會透過 iOS 內建的分享功能分享報告。

**刪除資料。** 在「報告」分頁按「清除所有紀錄」，或刪除 App。所有資料都存在你的裝置上。

**兒童。** 本 App 不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。

**不是醫療器材。** 小心台階是提醒工具，不能取代拐杖、陪伴的人，或你自己的注意力。

**聯絡方式。** 請在 https://github.com/tel0955850213/mindthestep/issues 留言。
