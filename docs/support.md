# Support · 支援

## English

**What you need:** an iPhone with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro models). The family note needs Apple Intelligence.

**How to wear it:** hang the iPhone on a lanyard at chest height, or clip it to a cane, tilted forward so the camera sees the ground 1–2 m ahead.

**It says "Tilt phone forward":** the phone is pointing too far down or up. Aim at the ground about 1–2 m ahead.

**It says "Can't see the ground":** something is blocking the view, or you are too close to a wall or furniture. It will not guess when it can't see.

**Family alerts:** open the Family tab, tap "Send setup link", and send it to your family member. They open the link in a browser, or subscribe in the free ntfy app.

**Questions or bugs:** https://github.com/tel0955850213/mindthestep/issues

MindTheStep is an awareness aid, not a medical device.

## 繁體中文

**需要什麼：** 有 LiDAR 的 iPhone（iPhone 12 Pro 以後的 Pro 機型）。家人短訊需要 Apple Intelligence。

**怎麼配戴：** 用掛繩把 iPhone 掛在胸口高度，或夾在拐杖上，稍微往前傾，讓鏡頭看到前方 1–2 公尺的地面。

**出現「手機往前傾」：** 手機太朝下或太朝上，請對準前方 1–2 公尺的地面。

**出現「看不到地面」：** 視線被擋住，或離牆壁、家具太近。看不清楚時，App 不會亂猜。

**家人通知：** 打開「家人」分頁，按「傳設定連結」，傳給家人。家人用瀏覽器打開連結，或在免費的 ntfy App 訂閱即可。

**問題回報：** https://github.com/tel0955850213/mindthestep/issues

小心台階是提醒工具，不是醫療器材。
